01

Profile & research focus

Thinking across disciplines.

My work connects statistical learning, the physics of intelligent systems, and decision-making in financial markets.

I am currently a Quantitative Researcher at Turing Fund Management. My research passion lies at the intersection of AI and statistical theories, with a specific focus on scaling laws, statistical mechanics, and reinforcement learning in finance.

Previously, I obtained my Ph.D. in Statistics from Shanghai University of Finance and Economics. Outside academics, I care deeply about literature, art, psychoanalysis, movies, and jazz. It would be a pleasure to connect and exchange ideas with you.
